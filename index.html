Our story

From a favor for a neighbor to SWFL’s trusted pool team

Aquatic Pool Services started the way the best small businesses do — with one person who simply cared.

Before pools, Jerritt Swope spent his days at the Dunes Golf Course, where taking pride in the little details was second nature. When neighbors and customers needed a hand keeping their pools clean and balanced, he stepped in — not as a business, just as a favor.

Word got around. One pool became a handful, a handful became a route, and a simple act of helping out grew into a thriving, family-owned business. More than twenty years later, Jerritt and his wife Christa have cared for over 300 pools across Southwest Florida — and they still treat every one like it belongs to a neighbor.

Today it’s still a true family operation — Jerritt, Christa, and the team are the same trusted faces every week, the kind of people you’re comfortable handing a house key to while you’re up north. That’s why so many customers have stayed with them for years.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

A few things homeowners ask before they reach out.

What’s included in weekly pool service?

Every visit we skim, brush, and vacuum, empty the skimmer and pump baskets, and run a full water test — balancing chlorine, pH, alkalinity, and stabilizer — then check that your pump and equipment are running right.

How much does pool service cost?

Every pool is a little different, so we give a free, no-obligation quote based on your pool and what you need. The fastest way to get a price is to text or call (239) 357-6622.

What areas do you serve?

Fort Myers, Iona, Bonita Springs, Estero, Sanibel Island, and the surrounding Southwest Florida communities. Not sure if we reach you? Just ask.

Can you look after my pool and home while I’m away?

Absolutely — that’s one of our specialties. We keep your pool swim-ready year-round, check on your home, pick up the mail, and handle pre- and post-storm inspections, so seasonal residents come back to everything just as they left it.

My pool turned green — can you fix it?

Yes. Our green-to-clean recovery brings cloudy, algae-filled water back to crystal clear, and then we keep it that way with regular service.

Do you repair pool equipment?

We do — pumps, filters, heaters, and salt cells. We clean, diagnose, and repair so everything keeps running, and we can take care of storm cleanup too.
